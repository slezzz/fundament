Fundament privacy

Fundament checks pages on this device. Analysis stays on the device unless you confirm a public false-alarm report.

What can leave the device

  • Phishing list updates, at most every 6 hours: one GET of a signed file. No cookies, no browsing data, no identifiers.
  • Chrome version check, at most every 6 hours: GET https://versionhistory.googleapis.com/v1/chrome/platforms/<platform>/channels/stable/versions/all/releases?filter=endtime=none without cookies. Nothing about you is in the request.
  • Have I Been Pwned, only when you run it: a 5-character prefix of a SHA-1 is sent to api.pwnedpasswords.com. The password is cleared before the request.

This build has no telemetry switch and no address for counters.

Permissions

  • storage — settings, lists, and a short local event log (24 hours, max 40 entries).
  • tabs — the URL of the tab being analyzed.
  • webNavigation — main-frame navigations, including redirects.
  • declarativeNetRequest — block tracker and blocklisted requests without reading bodies.
  • declarativeNetRequestFeedback — count which rules matched.
  • alarms — trim caches and the event log.
  • downloads — see download URLs and filenames. Files are not uploaded.
  • privacy — harden WebRTC, prefetch, and hyperlink auditing.
  • contentSettings — optional block of notification prompts. Off by default.
  • contextMenus — allow, block, or pause the current site.
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess — privacy headers and tracking-parameter stripping.
  • management — list other extensions. Fundament does not disable them.
  • notifications — a local notice when a download was stopped.
  • Host access http://*/*, https://*/* — page checks and the warning screen. The address is not sent to a server.

This build has no externally_connectable entry. warning.html can be opened by a redirect. A site can probe that URL and learn the extension is installed. It cannot read anything from it.
