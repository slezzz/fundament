Fundament — third-party data and software
==========================================

Fundament itself is released under the MIT License (see LICENSE in the source repository).
It ships the following third-party data. None of it is fetched at runtime.

1. Phishing.Database — rules/threats-feed.json
   https://github.com/mitchellkrogza/Phishing.Database
   License: MIT
   Copyright (c) Mitchell Krog and contributors.
   Fundament filters the list before shipping (platform, shortener, and popular hosts are
   removed so that a single bad URL cannot block a whole service).

2. Public Suffix List — compiled into background.js and the UI bundles
   https://publicsuffix.org/
   License: Mozilla Public License, v. 2.0 (https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/)
   The list is included unmodified apart from punycode conversion of IDN rules.

3. Majestic Million — data/popular-domains.txt, data/hosted-zones.txt (derived)
   https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
   Attribution: "Majestic Million" by Majestic-12 Ltd, used under CC BY 3.0.
   Changes: reduced to the top 50,000 registrable domains; hosted-zones.txt is derived by
   intersecting it with the phishing list.
